Broad Money in Five Asian Economies, 2011-2023

What moves with broad money?

    Generated from the data and the indicator, year and countries selected above.

    Data and methods

    • Countries and period: Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Thailand and Vietnam, annual data for 2011-2023.
    • Broad money: broad money as a percentage of GDP (World Bank). This is the dependent variable.
    • FDI: net foreign direct investment inflows, balance of payments, current US$ (World Bank), expressed as a natural logarithm.
    • Interest rate: policy rate in percent, from each country's central bank.
    • Trade openness: trade as a percentage of GDP (World Bank).
    • Method: descriptive comparison of the indicators, plus Pearson correlation between each explanatory variable and broad money, across all country-years and within each country over time.
